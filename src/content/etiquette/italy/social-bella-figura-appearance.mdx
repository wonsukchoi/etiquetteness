summary: Putting together a neat, deliberate outfit — even for a short errand like buying bread — matters more in Italy than in many other cultures; showing up sciatto (sloppy) reflects on how seriously people take you.
context: >
  Known as fare bella figura ("making a good impression/figure"), the
  idea extends beyond clothing to general presentation and how you
  carry a social situation — it's less about fashion trends and more
  about looking intentional rather than careless.
source: Italian social etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02

Choose put-together, well-fitted clothing over athletic wear or visibly worn-out clothes for most everyday outings — gym clothes are for the gym, not for running errands in town.

The same instinct applies more broadly: showing up prepared, on time, and presentable to a social or business occasion is part of bella figura, not just what you're wearing.
