Choose put-together, well-fitted clothing over athletic wear or visibly worn-out clothes for most everyday outings, gym clothes are for the gym, not for running errands in town.

The same instinct applies more broadly: showing up prepared, on time, and presentable to a social or business occasion is part of bella figura, not just what you're wearing.

A few concrete examples of the concept in action:

- **Discreet bill-paying** — the bella figura way to pay for a group meal is to quietly slip away from the table and settle up out of sight, rather than handling money in front of your guests.
- **Generous hosting** — offering food or coffee to any visitor, even a brief one, and apologizing for the meal's "shortcomings" even when the table is full, is a hosting reflex, not literal modesty.
- **A brutta figura moment** looks like the opposite: complaining about family in public, or visibly losing composure (too much to drink, an outburst) in front of others.
