---
title: Don't order a cappuccino after 11am
country: italy
category: dining
severity: casual
summary: Cappuccino (and other milky coffees) are treated as a breakfast-only drink in Italy — ordering one after a meal, especially dinner, marks you as a tourist and is seen as odd for digestion.
context: >
  The reasoning given is that a large dose of milk after a meal is
  considered heavy on the stomach. Espresso, by contrast, is the
  standard after-meal order at any hour.
source: Italian dining etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Order cappuccino, latte, or other milk-based coffee only in the morning — Italians largely stop by late morning, well before lunch.

After any meal, order an espresso instead. No one will stop you from ordering a cappuccino at 4pm, but expect it to register as a small tell that you're not local.
