If you're a man, don't be shy about making the first move — asking for the date, holding the door, and reaching for the bill are all expected gestures. If you're used to splitting the bill, expect a different default here.

Compliments and charm are part of the culture. Expect some flattery, and enjoy the courtship rather than rushing it.
