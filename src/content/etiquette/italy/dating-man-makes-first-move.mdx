If you're a man, don't be shy about making the first move — asking for the date, choosing the place, and reaching for the bill are all expected gestures. If you're a woman, it's fine to signal interest, but many Italians still appreciate the man taking the lead.

Compliments and charm are part of the culture. A genuine compliment about appearance or style is normal and welcome, not over-familiar.
