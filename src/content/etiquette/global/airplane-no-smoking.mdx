No smoking of any kind (cigarettes, vapes, e-cigarettes) anywhere on a commercial aircraft, including the lavatories. This applies for the entire flight, not just takeoff/landing.

Disabling or tampering with a lavatory smoke detector to hide vaping is treated as a serious security violation, not just an etiquette breach, and can result in the flight being diverted.
