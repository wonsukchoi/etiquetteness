title: Never smoke on a commercial flight
country: global
category: travel
severity: strict
summary: Smoking, including e-cigarettes, is banned on virtually all commercial flights worldwide.
context: >
  Airlines banned smoking industry-wide starting in the late 1980s–1990s over
  fire risk and cabin air quality. It's now enforced as a hard rule, not a
  courtesy — tampering with a lavatory smoke detector is a federal offense in
  many countries.
source: International civil aviation norms
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

No smoking of any kind — cigarettes, vapes, e-cigarettes — anywhere on a commercial aircraft, including the lavatories. This applies for the entire flight, not just takeoff/landing.

Disabling or tampering with a lavatory smoke detector to hide vaping is treated as a serious security violation, not just an etiquette breach, and can result in the flight being diverted.
