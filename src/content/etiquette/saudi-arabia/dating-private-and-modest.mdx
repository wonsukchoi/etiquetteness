Keep the relationship out of public view. No hand-holding, kissing, or intimate gestures in public, and be mindful of how you dress and behave in shared spaces.

Expect dating to be more formal and family-oriented. Serious relationships typically move toward family involvement and marriage, so be clear about your intentions and respectful of conservative norms.
