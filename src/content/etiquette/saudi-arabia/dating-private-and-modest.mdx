summary: In Saudi Arabia, dating is a private matter aimed at marriage — couples rarely show affection in public and usually get to know each other discreetly, in neutral public places or online.
context: >
  "Dating" in Saudi Arabia generally means getting to know someone with
  the prospect of marriage in mind. It is rare for men and women to show
  open affection in public, and many couples are not permitted to see
  one another alone, so they usually meet in neutral public places or
  online. Marriages are often arranged by relatives or a matchmaker,
  though in cities it is becoming more common for young people to tell
  their parents who they are interested in.

Keep the relationship out of public view. Open affection between men and women is rare in public, so no hand-holding, kissing, or intimate gestures.

Expect dating to be more formal and family-oriented. Serious relationships move toward family involvement and marriage, so be clear about your intentions and respectful of conservative norms.
