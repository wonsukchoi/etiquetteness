Cover shoulders and knees at minimum in public; looser, non-revealing clothing is the safer default for both men and women, particularly outside international hotels and tourist zones.

When meeting someone of the opposite gender, don't extend your hand first — a nod, a hand over the heart, or a verbal greeting works, and you can shake hands if they offer theirs.
