---
title: Don't schedule business around Friday or prayer times
country: saudi-arabia
category: business
severity: strict
summary: The weekend is Friday–Saturday, not Saturday–Sunday, and Friday midday is reserved for communal prayer, avoid scheduling meetings then, and expect offices, shops, and meetings to pause briefly around each of the five daily prayer times.
context: >
  Friday (Jumu'ah) is the main congregational prayer day in Islam.
  Businesses commonly close or slow down for it, and many shops and
  offices close for short windows during each daily prayer, plan
  meeting times with buffer around them rather than back-to-back.
source: Saudi Arabian business etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Plan around a Friday–Saturday weekend, with Sunday as a normal working day. Avoid booking anything during Friday midday prayer specifically; it's the one most businesses actively close for.

Across the rest of the week, build slack into your schedule around the five daily prayer times, since shops, restaurants, and even meetings may pause briefly when the call to prayer sounds.
