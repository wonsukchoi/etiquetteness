  Purple carries a funeral association in Brazil the way black often
  does elsewhere, so a purple bow or purple flowers on an otherwise

  their own symbolic problem: giving something that cuts is read as
  an unspoken wish to cut the relationship, regardless of how
  practical the gift actually is. Neither rule is about the gift's
  value, both are about the specific symbol landing wrong at a
  moment meant to be warm.

When choosing wrapping paper or flowers, lean toward bright, vivid colors — yellow and green (Brazil's national colors) are always a safe, well-received choice. Save purple for occasions that call for it, not a birthday or hostess gift.

Steer clear of knives, scissors, or anything else with a blade as a gift, no matter how useful the recipient might find it. A small chocolate, a bottle of wine, or orchids are all standard, low-risk choices instead.
