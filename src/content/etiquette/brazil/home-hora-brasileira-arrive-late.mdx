  it would elsewhere. Business meetings don't follow this same
  looseness, so treat "hora brasileira" as a home-and-social-only rule.

For a dinner, party, or casual invitation to someone's home, plan to arrive around 20 minutes after the time you were given, not before. Showing up early or exactly on time isn't wrong, but it's unusual enough that your host may still be mid-preparation.

Bring a small gift when you're invited (see the gift-giving entry for what to avoid), and don't treat this looser sense of time as license to be dramatically late — 20 minutes is the norm, not an hour.
