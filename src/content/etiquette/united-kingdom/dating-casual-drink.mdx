Suggest a pub, a café, or a walk for the first date. It keeps things relaxed and gives you room to talk.

Keep the tone light. Good-natured banter goes down better than grand romantic gestures, and turning up with flowers on a first date is too much.

Don't count on splitting the bill. The man typically pays on the first date, and the usual way to even things out is for the other person to pay next time.
