Suggest a pub, bar, or café for the first date. It keeps things relaxed and gives both of you an easy exit. Splitting the bill is standard — offering to split is polite, not rude.

Keep the tone light and self-deprecating. Brits appreciate understatement and humor over grand romantic gestures on a first date.
