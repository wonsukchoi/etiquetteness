Ask "should I take my shoes off?" when you arrive rather than assuming, it's a genuinely split norm here, and asking costs nothing.

Say yes the first time you're offered a drink if you actually want one, offers aren't always repeated, so a polite "oh, I'm fine" the first time can mean you don't get asked again. If tea's offered and you'd rather have coffee or water, it's completely fine to just say so.

Bring a modest gift for a meal or party invitation, wine, a box of chocolates, or flowers all work, and a packet of biscuits is plenty for a quick tea visit. Skip the elaborate hamper, it reads as overdone rather than generous for anything short of a special occasion.

- **A cup of tea offered unusually late in the evening is worth a second thought** — it's sometimes a genuine offer, and sometimes a very gentle signal that it might be time to head home.
