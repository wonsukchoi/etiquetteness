Gold, red, and other bright colors are safe, celebratory choices for gift wrap. Save black and white for their actual context (mourning dress and funeral-related items) rather than everyday gifts.

Hand the gift over with your right hand, or both hands for something more formal, and don't be surprised if it's set aside to open later rather than opened immediately in front of you.
