title: Don't touch someone's head or point with your feet
country: thailand
category: dating
severity: strict
summary: In Thailand, the head is sacred and the feet are lowly — never touch a date's head, and never point at them with your feet, even playfully.
context: >
  In Thailand the top of the head is considered the most sacred part of
  the body, and the feet the dirtiest. You should never touch someone on
  the top of their head, and feet should not be used to point at things
  or move objects, with the soles never pointed at another person.
  Couples are also reserved in public — displays of affection such as
  holding hands or kissing are generally not shown.

Never reach for your date's head, even to ruffle their hair playfully. Sit so that the soles of your feet don't point at your partner, and don't use a foot to point at or move things.

Keep physical affection private. Couples in Thailand generally don't hold hands or kiss in public.
