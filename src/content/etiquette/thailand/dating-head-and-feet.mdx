title: Don't touch someone's head or point with your feet
country: thailand
category: dating
severity: strict
summary: In Thailand, the head is sacred and the feet are lowly — never touch a date's head, and never point at them with your feet, even playfully.
context: >
  Thai culture holds the head as the most sacred part of the body and
  the feet as the lowest. Touching someone's head — even affectionately —
  is deeply disrespectful, and pointing with your feet is a serious
  insult. On a date, keep these boundaries in mind to avoid offending
  your partner.
source: "Culture Trip: Thai etiquette"
sourceUrl: https://theculturetrip.com/asia/thailand/articles/thai-etiquette
updatedAt: 2026-09-06
---

Never reach for your date's head, even to ruffle their hair or playfully. Keep your feet off furniture and never point them at your partner.

Public displays of affection are also more restrained in Thailand than in the West. Holding hands is fine, but kissing in public is generally frowned upon.
