  attempting it is well received.

For most everyday greetings, press your palms together near your chest and give a small bow of the head — you'll see this returned in kind by most people you meet.

For monks or much older/senior people, a slightly higher hand position and deeper bow shows more respect. As a visitor, a simple, sincere wai is enough — precision matters far less than the gesture itself.
