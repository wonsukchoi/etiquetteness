Wear a top with sleeves and bottoms that go past the knee, for men and women alike, before heading to a temple. Sleeveless shirts, tank tops, shorts, and short skirts are the most common reasons visitors get stopped at the entrance.

Take your shoes off before stepping inside any temple building, leave them with the others at the entrance rather than looking for a special spot.

- **Bring a scarf or light wrap if you're not sure your outfit will pass** — many temples sell or lend one at the entrance, but having your own saves time and avoids relying on availability.
- **Major, royally connected temples enforce this most strictly** — Wat Phra Kaew (Temple of the Emerald Buddha) at the Grand Palace in Bangkok is the standard example; smaller local temples may be more relaxed, but it's safest to dress the same way everywhere.
