If you're buying something specifically as a gift, get it wrapped at the store rather than wrapping it yourself — department store wrapping paper in particular reads as a quality signal, not just packaging.

Present and receive the gift with both hands, with a slight bow as you do. Say thank you, then set it aside to open later rather than tearing into it on the spot, especially in a group or business setting.

- **This is more relaxed among close friends** — a casual gift between friends who already open things in front of each other is fine; the formality scales with the setting.
- **If the giver insists you open it now, that overrides the default** — some people do want to see your reaction, so read the moment rather than treating "wait to open it" as an absolute rule.
