If you're interested in someone in Japan, don't rely on subtle hints or assume mutual interest. Make your feelings clear with a direct confession — in person is most traditional, though a message is increasingly accepted.

Be prepared for a clear yes or no. The kokuhaku is meant to remove guesswork, so a direct answer (even a rejection) is considered respectful rather than harsh.
