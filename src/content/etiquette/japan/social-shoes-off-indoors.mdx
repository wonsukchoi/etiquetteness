title: Take your shoes off indoors
country: japan

summary: Remove your shoes at the entrance (genkan) of homes, and at many traditional restaurants, ryokan, and temples, step into the indoor slippers provided, and never walk onto tatami mats in slippers either.
context: >

  and clean indoor space in Japanese homes. Look for a step up in floor
  level and a shoe rack or slipper set as the visual cue that you've
  reached one.

Take your shoes off at the genkan (the lowered entryway) and step up into the house in socks or bare feet, then into the slippers provided if there are any.

Tatami-mat rooms are a further step down: slippers come off again before you walk on tatami. There are often separate slippers just for the bathroom, don't wear those back out into the rest of the house.
