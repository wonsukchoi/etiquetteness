---
title: Exchange business cards with two hands
country: japan
category: business
severity: strict
summary: Present and receive meishi (business cards) with both hands, and read the card before putting it away.
context: >
  The meishi exchange is a small ritual of mutual respect rooted in Japanese
  business culture. Handling the card carelessly, one-handed, stuffing it in a
  back pocket, or writing on it, reads as disrespect toward the person, not
  just the paper.
source: Japanese business etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Offer your card with both hands, text facing the recipient. Receive theirs the same way, take a moment to actually read it, and place it on the table in front of you (or in a card holder) during the meeting, not straight into a pocket or wallet.

Never write on someone's card in front of them, and never put it in your back pocket, since that's read as sitting on it.
