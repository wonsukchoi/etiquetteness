  efficient wording that is normal in English can read as blunt, and mixing

  you for your continued support", as glossed in Toranomon Language

Start with the set greeting いつもお世話になっております, then give your company and name, and finish with よろしくお願いいたします, the standard short closing. Write the recipient's line in the order company, department, name, with 様 after the name.

- **Add 様 to every name.** For a department or team with no named person, use 御中 instead, and never put 御中 after a personal name.
- **Make the subject line specific.** Aim for about 20 characters that say what the email is about before it is opened: 〇〇社 見積もり確認依頼 ("please confirm the quote") works better than 〇〇社の件 ("about company X").
- **Keep お疲れ様です for colleagues.** It's an internal greeting between co-workers and shouldn't be used with people outside your company.
- **Skip emoticons and avoid odd hours.** The source suggests sending between 8:30 a.m. and 7:00 p.m.
- **End with a signature block.** Company and department, your name, address, phone number and email address.
