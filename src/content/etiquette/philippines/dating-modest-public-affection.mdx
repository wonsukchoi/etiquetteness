When dating in the Philippines, keep public affection moderate. Holding hands, walking arm in arm, sitting close — these are fine and normal. What you should avoid is heavy PDA: extended passionate kissing or intimate touching in public, which reads as inappropriate in this predominantly Catholic, conservative culture and can draw open disapproval.

Being mindful isn't coldness — it's reading the room and matching local norms. Public settings, families around, and church or formal environments all call for extra restraint. Save the more intimate displays for private moments, and you'll show respect for the culture and for the person you're with.

- **Light PDA (hand-holding, closeness) is fine**; heavy or intimate affection is not.
- **Be extra reserved around families and in formal/religious settings.**
