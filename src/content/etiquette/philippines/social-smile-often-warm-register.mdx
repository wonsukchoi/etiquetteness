In everyday Philippine interactions, a smile is the baseline. Greet people warmly, smile easily, and keep a light, friendly tone — it signals openness and a readiness to get along, which is exactly the register Filipinos operate in. Shopkeepers, colleagues, and strangers all default to a friendly smile.

That doesn't mean a forced grin; it means matching the warm, easygoing tone of the people around you. When you genuinely engage and smile, the warmth is usually returned in kind. A persistent straight face can come across as annoyed or standoffish even when you don't mean it that way.

- **Smiling is the default politeness marker**, not a sign of amusement or insincerity.
- **Match the easygoing register** — reserve serious or stern expressions for genuinely serious moments.
