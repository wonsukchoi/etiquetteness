In a Filipino home, expect to be offered food or drink more than once. The first polite "no, thank you" is conventional, but when the host offers again — and they usually will — that's the sincere invitation, and accepting it is the polite path. Taking a little of what's offered honors the host's generosity.

The same principle extends to help, seating, or other gestures: a persistent guest who keeps refusing can make the host feel their hospitality is unwanted. Accept with a warm thank you ("salamat po"), enjoy it, and the meal or visit unfolds naturally.

- **The second offer is the real one** — accept there rather than holding out a third refusal.
- **Take only a small portion** if you're not hungry; accepting the token gesture matters more than consuming everything.
