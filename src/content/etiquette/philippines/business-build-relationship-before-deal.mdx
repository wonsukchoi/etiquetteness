At a Filipino business meeting, expect a warm, unhurried opening: small talk about family, health, or shared contacts, often for several minutes, before anyone raises the agenda. Lean into this rather than redirecting it — it's the actual work of building the relationship your business will sit on.

Don't treat personal questions as intrusive curiosity. Asking about a colleague's family or how their weekend was is normal rapport-building, and reciprocating warmly is expected. Getting to the point too quickly is the more common visitor misstep, since it reads as impatience with the relationship itself.

- **A calm, unhurried pace signals respect**, not inefficiency — trying to speed things up can damage rapport.
- **The relationship is expected to outlast the deal**; decisions often hinge on the quality of the working relationship more than the specific terms.
