When you wrap a gift for a Philippine celebration, lean on bright and warm colors — red, gold, and other festive shades — and generally avoid white and black, which are tied to mourning and funeral dress. This is a casual rather than strict rule, and most people won't take deep offense, but matching the wrapping to the joy of the occasion is a nice touch.

Pay the same attention to content: a bouquet of white flowers, for instance, can carry a similar funeral association, so for a celebratory event opt for brighter or more colorful blooms. When in doubt, warm or neutral tones with an accent of red are a safe, festive choice.

- **Red and gold read as lucky and celebratory** in Philippine culture — great defaults for wrapping.
- **The same caution applies to flower color**, not just paper: bright, mixed bouquets for celebrations, white reserved for sympathy.
