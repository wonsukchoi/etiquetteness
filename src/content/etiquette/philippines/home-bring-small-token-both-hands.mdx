When you visit a Filipino home, bring something small: food, a bottle of wine or beer, a dessert, or a modest token. Present it with both hands as you greet the host — it signals respect and sincere good intent, and it's a gesture Filipinos appreciate.

Keep the gift practical and shareable rather than extravagant — a dish you contribute naturally becomes part of the meal or gathering. Don't be offended if the host thanks you but doesn't open it immediately; that's normal. And when the host protests that you shouldn't have, a light "it's nothing, just something small" resolves it gracefully.

- **Consumables fit the culture best** — food and drink are shared, so the gift becomes part of the hospitality.
- **Present with both hands, slightly bowed** — a small but meaningful mark of respect.
