Riding a jeepney: wait at a stop, flag one down, and board. As you get on, state your destination loudly so the driver hears. Your fare — hand it forward along the chain of passengers toward the driver; change comes back the same way. When you need to alight, signal well before your stop, typically by tapping a coin on the rail or calling "para po" loudly.

Have small bills ready, since drivers carry limited change. If you're unsure about the route or fare, other passengers are usually friendly and happy to help — asking is normal and welcomed.

- **State your destination clearly when boarding** so the driver can confirm the route.
- **Signal early for your stop** — "para po" with a raised coin or a clear shout is the standard cue.
