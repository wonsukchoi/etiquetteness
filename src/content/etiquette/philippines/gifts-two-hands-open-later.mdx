Hand over a gift with both hands rather than one, this applies whether it's a personal or business gift, formal or casual.

Don't expect the recipient to open it on the spot, and don't feel obligated to open one you've received right away either. If you're the giver and it seems like the recipient is unsure whether to open it, a quick "you can open it later" removes the ambiguity.

- **This is a soft norm, not a strict one** — plenty of casual exchanges between friends involve opening a gift immediately, especially with younger or more Westernized circles.
- **Pair this with the flower and wrapping guidance** — the same gift-giving occasion often involves all three: two-handed presentation, careful wrapping, and no pressure to open immediately.
