When you enter a Philippine church, dress with modesty — cover shoulders and knees — and remove your hat. Keep conversation to a whisper or silence, turn off your phone's ringer, and avoid walking across the front of the altar during a service.

Visiting quietly as a tourist is generally fine and welcomed, but be sensitive to those praying: don't pose for loud photo sessions in the middle of Mass, and respect any posted rules about photography. A little extra reverence costs nothing and honors a tradition that means a great deal locally.

- **Cover shoulders and knees** as a firm baseline for church visits.
- **Photos: be discreet** — ask or avoid shooting during services, and respect "no photo" notices.
