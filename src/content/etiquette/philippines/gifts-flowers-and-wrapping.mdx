If you're bringing flowers to a home visit or a colleague, most varieties are fine, just steer clear of lilies and chrysanthemums specifically, since both read as funeral flowers rather than a warm gesture.

Spend a little extra effort on how the gift is wrapped, whether that's flowers, food, or something else. A nicely wrapped modest gift tends to land better than an expensive gift wrapped carelessly, presentation is doing real work here, not just the contents.

- **Books, small electronics, or something distinctive from your own country** are all safe, well-received choices for a business or personal gift.
- **Avoid anything too extravagant** — an overly lavish gift can create discomfort or an unwanted sense of obligation rather than just being appreciated.
