In public, dress neatly and lean modest. Everyday life — markets, streets, casual dining — accommodates shorts, t-shirts, and sandals, but avoid clothing that's extremely revealing in general settings. For workplaces, formal events, and especially church or religious occasions, cover shoulders and knees and dress a step smarter.

Smart casual is a reliable default for most social and business gatherings: a collared shirt or blouse and clean trousers rarely miss. When in doubt for a specific event, dressing slightly more formally than the situation seems to require is safer than under-dressing.

- **Modesty spikes around church and formal events** — shoulders and knees covered there as a rule.
- **Neatness reads as respect**; a tidy, put-together appearance is noticed and appreciated.
