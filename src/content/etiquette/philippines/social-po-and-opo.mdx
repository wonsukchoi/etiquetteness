When you answer a question from an elder or a superior, end your sentence with "po" — for example, "Salamat po" (thank you) or "Opo" (yes, respectfully).

You don't need to use it with friends or people your own age, but when in doubt with someone older, adding "po" is always the safer, more respectful choice.
