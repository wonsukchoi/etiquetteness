When dating in the Philippines, expect the family to be part of the picture. A partner's relatives will often take a genuine interest in you, and being warm, respectful, and polite with them — using "po"/"opo" with elders, greeting them properly — is a big part of making the relationship work.

Meeting the family may come earlier than in many Western cultures, sometimes well before engagement. Don't be surprised if a weekend at the family home or a big family gathering is on the agenda. Treat it as a meaningful, positive step rather than an early commitment, because that's how it's understood locally.

- **Respect for the family is respect for the relationship** — warmth with relatives matters enormously.
- **Family approval often weighs heavily** on whether a relationship goes the distance.
