As you travel, greet people warmly and often — a smile and a polite greeting go a long way, and shopkeepers, drivers, and strangers are generally friendly and glad to help. If something goes wrong (a mix-up, a disagreement, a misunderstanding about a fare), keep your voice low and your tone calm. Patience and a light touch resolve most situations far better than confrontation.

Public displays of anger are deeply off-putting in Philippine culture and will mark you as impolite. If you feel frustrated, step back, breathe, and re-approach calmly — it's both the respectful and the more effective path. When in doubt, a smile and an apology defuse almost anything.

- **Stay calm even when things go wrong** — raising your voice is seen as a personal attack, not problem-solving.
- **A smile defuses tension** better than any argument when there's a misunderstanding.
