---
title: Skip chrysanthemums and even numbers when gifting flowers
country: france
category: gifts
severity: strict
summary: When bringing flowers as a gift, avoid chrysanthemums (associated with funerals and cemetery visits) and avoid giving an even number of stems.
context: >
  Chrysanthemums are specifically tied to Toussaint (All Saints' Day)
  grave visits in French culture, so gifting them elsewhere reads as a
  bad omen. Even-numbered bouquets are also traditionally associated
  with funerals, odd numbers are the safe default (avoid 13 too).
source: A simple guide to gift giving etiquette in France – Annie André
sourceUrl: https://www.annieandre.com/a-guide-to-gift-giving-and-etiquette-in-france/
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Choose an odd number of stems, and steer clear of chrysanthemums specifically, most florists in France will already assume you don't want them for a gift bouquet unless you ask.

Bring flowers already arranged rather than wrapped raw, and hand them over unwrapped or loosely wrapped, arriving with a giant floral cellophane cone reads as overly formal for a casual dinner invitation.
