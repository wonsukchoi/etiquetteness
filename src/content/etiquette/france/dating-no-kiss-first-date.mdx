summary: In France, a kiss on the lips usually signals that the two of you are now exclusive, so don't treat it as a casual way to end a first date.
context: >
  In France, a kiss on the lips indicates that a couple has become
  exclusive to each other unless they agree otherwise. Women generally
  expect men to initiate the relationship. It is also common for a woman
  to go to dinner with a male friend whether or not they are dating, so
  a dinner invitation is not proof of romantic interest by itself. Many
  people keep a new relationship private, without introducing each
  other to family and friends, until a significant amount of time has
  passed.

Don't assume a first date ends with a kiss, and don't push for one. Because a kiss on the lips signals that you're now together, it carries more weight here than a casual goodnight kiss.

Don't read too much into a dinner invitation, and don't be surprised if you aren't introduced to family or friends for a while. If someone no longer wants to be in the relationship, expect to be told directly.
