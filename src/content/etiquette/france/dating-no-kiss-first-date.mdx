End the date warmly but don't assume a kiss is expected. A hand on the shoulder, a lingering goodbye, or a "I'd love to see you again" all signal interest without pressure.

If you do go for a kiss, let it be mutual — lean in slowly and pause, giving the other person room to meet you or pull back. A rejected kiss is far more awkward than a missed one.
