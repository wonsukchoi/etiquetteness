---
title: Build rapport before getting to business
country: mexico
category: business
severity: casual
summary: Spend the first few minutes of a meeting on small talk (family, travel, how someone is doing) before moving to the agenda. Jumping straight to business reads as cold or transactional.
context: >
  Personal relationship (confianza) is treated as the foundation business
  gets built on, not a distraction from it. Meetings that skip the
  warm-up can leave a Mexican counterpart feeling like the relationship
  wasn't valued, even if the business content is identical.
source: The Mexican Business Communication Style – Commisceo Global
sourceUrl: https://commisceo-global.com/articles/the-mexican-communication-style/
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Let the host or most senior person set the pace, they'll typically open with some personal conversation before turning to the agenda. Match that energy rather than steering straight to the topic at hand.

This extends across the relationship, not just the first meeting: checking in personally before diving into a request (even by email) tends to land better than a purely transactional message.
