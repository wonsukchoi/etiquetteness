---
title: Skip marigolds as an everyday gift flower
country: mexico
category: gifts
severity: casual
summary: Avoid giving marigolds (cempasúchil) as a casual gift bouquet — they're specifically associated with Día de los Muertos altars and grave decoration.
context: >
  Marigolds are the signature flower of Día de los Muertos, used to
  guide spirits to ofrendas and graves with their scent and color.
  Giving them outside that context can read as an odd, funeral-adjacent
  choice rather than a cheerful gift.
source: Mexican gift-giving etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Roses, gerberas, or other bright non-marigold flowers are safe defaults for a casual gift. Save marigolds for their intended season and context around Día de los Muertos (late October–early November), when they're expected and meaningful rather than out of place.

As elsewhere, odd numbers of stems are generally the safer choice for a gift bouquet.
