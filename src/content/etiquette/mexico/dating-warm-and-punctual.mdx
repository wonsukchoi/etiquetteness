summary: In Mexico, dating is warm and expressive — chivalry and romantic gestures matter, affection in public is normal, and the approval of a partner's parents is very important.
context: >
  Chivalry is important throughout the dating process in Mexico,
  especially in more conservative and religious circles. Men are
  expected to behave like gentlemen and to charm their date with
  romantic gestures such as flowers, paying for her expenses, and gifts,
  while women often act aloof at first. Flattering comments, known as
  piropos, are common. Public displays of affection between couples are
  normal and acceptable, but are usually avoided in front of family
  while a couple is still dating. Parents' approval of a partner is very
  important, so couples usually meet at pre-arranged places until they
  are ready for family visits.

Be warm and attentive. If you're a man, small romantic gestures and picking up the bill are expected parts of courtship, and a sincere compliment is welcome.

Affection in public is fine, but hold back in front of your date's family while the relationship is new. Take meeting the parents seriously — their approval matters a great deal.
