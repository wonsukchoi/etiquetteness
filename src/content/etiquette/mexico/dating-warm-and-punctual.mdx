Arrive on time for the first date — it signals respect. Be warm and complimentary; Mexicans appreciate genuine charm and affection.

Don't rush to end the date. Mexicans value the connection and conversation, so let the evening breathe. A kiss on the cheek as a greeting or goodbye is common and friendly.
