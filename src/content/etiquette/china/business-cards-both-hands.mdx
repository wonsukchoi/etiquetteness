Hold your card by its top corners and present it with both hands, oriented so the recipient can read it right-side up without turning it.

Receive a card the same way, read it before setting it down, and keep it visible on the table during the meeting rather than pocketing it immediately.

- **Have your card translated into Chinese** (simplified characters) if you deal with China regularly — an English-only card reads as under-prepared.
- **Check your card is pristine before handing it over** — no bent corners, no smudges. A shabby card reflects on you, not just the printer.
- **Never write on a card you've just received**, in front of the giver or otherwise.
