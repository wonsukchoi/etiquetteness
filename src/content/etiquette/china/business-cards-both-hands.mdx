---
title: Present business cards with both hands
country: china
category: business
severity: strict
summary: Offer and receive business cards with both hands, text facing the recipient, and take a moment to read a card before putting it away.
context: >
  The card exchange stands in for a first impression of the person
  themselves, handling it carelessly (one hand, no glance, straight
  into a back pocket) reads as dismissive of the relationship, not just
  the paper.
source: Chinese business etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Hold your card by its top corners and present it with both hands, oriented so the recipient can read it right-side up without turning it.

Receive a card the same way, read it before setting it down, and keep it visible on the table during the meeting rather than pocketing it immediately.
