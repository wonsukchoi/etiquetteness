Once the top side of a whole fish is finished, lift the spine and bones out to reach the bottom fillet rather than turning the fish over.

At a shared table, this is usually done by the host or the most senior person present, not whoever happens to be closest.

- **The head is placed facing the guest of honor or the eldest person at the table** — don't rearrange the fish once it's been set down.
- **Leave the head and tail alone.** The whole, uncut fish symbolizes a complete and prosperous year; picking apart either end is thought to break that symbolism.
