---
title: Don't flip the whole fish over
country: china
category: dining
severity: strict
summary: When a whole fish is served and one side is finished, don't flip it over to eat the other side, lift the bone out instead.
context: >
  Common along coastal and fishing communities, where flipping a fish on
  the table is associated with a boat capsizing, bad luck for anyone
  present who works on or near the water. The custom has spread more
  broadly as a general dining taboo, especially at banquets.
source: Why It's Bad Etiquette To Flip Over A Whole Fish In China – Food Republic
sourceUrl: https://www.foodrepublic.com/1454556/bad-etiquette-flip-whole-fish-china/
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Once the top side of a whole fish is finished, lift the spine and bones out to reach the bottom fillet rather than turning the fish over.

At a shared table, this is usually done by the host or the most senior person present, not whoever happens to be closest.
