summary: In China, the traditional expectation is that the man pays on a date, but attitudes are changing and many younger women now prefer to split the bill.
context: >
  Many people in China still say it is only proper for the man to pay
  for his girlfriend on a date, and a man who doesn't can be seen as
  classless or stingy. At the same time, more young women who value
  their autonomy no longer want the man to pick up the bill, and a
  survey of university students found that both men and women hoped to
  share the cost of dating more equally than they actually did.
  Attitudes vary enough that it is hard to know in advance how an offer
  to pay will be received.

If you're a man, be ready to pay — it's still the default many people expect. If your date wants to go Dutch, take it at face value rather than insisting.

If you're a woman, offering to split or to pay your share is increasingly normal, especially among younger, university-educated people.
