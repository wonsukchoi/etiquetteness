---
title: Never give a clock as a gift
country: china
category: gifts
severity: strict
summary: Don't gift a clock, and avoid wrapping gifts in all-white or all-black paper, use red or gold instead.
context: >
  "Giving a clock" (送钟, sòng zhōng) sounds identical to a phrase meaning
  "attending a funeral" / "seeing someone off to their death" in
  Mandarin; the pun makes it a genuinely bad-luck gift, not just an odd
  one. White and black are funeral colors, while red and gold read as
  celebratory and auspicious.
source: "Beware of Chinese Taboos: Giving Gifts to Chinese People – DigMandarin"
sourceUrl: https://www.digmandarin.com/chinese-taboos-gifts.html
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Skip clocks and watches entirely as gifts, no matter how nice; the pun outweighs the thought. Sharp objects (knives, scissors) and handkerchiefs carry similar bad-luck associations and are worth avoiding too.

Wrap what you do give in red or gold paper. Present it with both hands, and don't be surprised if the recipient sets it aside without opening it in front of you, that's normal, not a snub.
