Wait to be told where to sit at a meeting or banquet table rather than taking a seat on your own, even if it seems like there's an obvious open spot. Your host is actively managing the arrangement, and it's saying something about rank whether you realize it or not.

If you're hosting, seat your most senior guest facing the door (the seat of honor), directly across from your own most senior person, then fill in the remaining seats by descending rank outward from there.

- **Let the most senior people speak first** in a meeting, and hold off on jumping into the conversation unless you're directly addressed, especially if you're junior on your side of the table.
- **This mirrors the seating hierarchy at a Chinese home dinner table** — same underlying logic, formalized further for a business setting.
