title: Eat and pass food with your right hand only
country: india
category: dining
severity: strict
summary: Use your right hand for eating, serving, and passing food or objects — the left hand is reserved for hygiene tasks and considered unclean at the table.
context: >
  This norm holds across much of South Asia regardless of religion. It
  applies beyond eating too — handing someone money, a gift, or an
  object with your left hand alone can land as impolite.
source: Indian dining etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Eat with your right hand, even if you're left-handed — many families teach left-handed children to eat right-handed specifically for this reason. If you need to steady a plate or serve yourself with a spoon, the left hand can assist, but food itself goes from right hand to mouth.

The same rule extends to handing over money, gifts, or documents — use your right hand, or both hands together for something formal.
