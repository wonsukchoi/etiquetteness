Offer a namaste (palms together, slight bow of the head) as your default greeting, particularly with older people or in situations where you're unsure whether a handshake would be welcome. Let the other person initiate a handshake if they want one.

In some families, younger members bend to briefly touch an elder's feet when greeting them at home or during festivals, and the elder responds with a blessing gesture. This is a family/regional custom rather than something expected of guests or strangers.
