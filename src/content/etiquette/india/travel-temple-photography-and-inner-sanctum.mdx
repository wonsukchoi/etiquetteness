Look for a posted sign before pulling out your phone or camera inside any temple, a lot of them prohibit photography and videography entirely, especially near the deity. If there's no sign and you're unsure, ask a priest or attendant rather than assuming it's fine.

Stick to the outer courtyard unless you're clearly invited further in. Many temples keep the inner sanctum open only to Hindus, and some restrict it further, to priests alone, who are the only ones permitted to approach or touch the idol.

- **If photography is allowed, keep it quick and discreet** — skip flash (it can damage old artwork) and don't stage an elaborate photoshoot in a space where people are actively worshipping.
- **This isn't a blanket "no visitors" rule** — most temples welcome people of all faiths into the main grounds, the restriction is specifically about the sanctum and the idol itself.
