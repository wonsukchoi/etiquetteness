summary: In India, the family is nearly always consulted before a marriage, so a relationship that turns serious is expected to involve the family.
context: >
  Arranged marriages are common throughout India, usually set up by a
  matchmaker, the couple's parents, or another trusted third party, and
  it is now more common for the family to ask the couple for their
  consent first. "Love marriages," where the couple choose each other,
  are becoming more common. However a couple meets, the family is nearly
  always consulted in the marriage process. Expectations vary by region
  and religion.

Don't treat a serious relationship as purely private. Expect to meet the family and be open about your intentions.

Be prepared for the family's view to carry real weight in whether, and how, the relationship moves toward marriage.
