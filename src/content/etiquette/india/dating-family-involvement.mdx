summary: In India, family approval matters — even in modern dating, meeting and winning over the family is an important step toward a serious relationship.
context: >
  Indian dating culture sits between modern courtship and traditional
  family involvement. While casual dating is common in cities, a
  relationship that turns serious is expected to involve the family.
  Meeting the parents, seeking their blessing, and being open about
  intentions are seen as signs of respect and seriousness.

Don't treat the relationship as purely private. Once things get serious, expect to meet the family and be open about your intentions. Being evasive about family is read as a lack of commitment.

Be respectful of conservative expectations, especially outside major cities. Public displays of affection are more restricted, and modesty in dress and behavior is appreciated.
