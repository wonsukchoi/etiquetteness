title: Avoid leather and white-wrapped gifts
country: india
category: gifts
severity: casual
summary: Skip leather goods as gifts (many Hindus avoid cow products) and avoid white or all-black wrapping, which reads as funeral-associated, and don't be surprised if your gift isn't opened in front of you.
context: >
  Cows hold religious significance in Hinduism, so leather items can be
  an unwelcome gift depending on the recipient's beliefs, bring
  something else unless you know them well. White is associated with
  mourning in many Indian communities, unlike its neutral status in the
  West.
source: Indian gift-giving etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

When unsure, sweets, fruit, or flowers (avoiding white ones) are safe defaults. Skip leather items unless you know the recipient doesn't observe the custom, and steer clear of frangipani or white flowers specifically, which are used at funerals.

Hand the gift over with your right hand or both hands. It's common for the recipient to set it aside unopened rather than unwrap it in front of you, that's polite restraint, not disinterest.
