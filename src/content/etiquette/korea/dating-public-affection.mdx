Keep things tasteful in public: holding hands, a hand on the shoulder, or a quick peck are fine. Save deeper affection for private settings.

Be especially mindful in more traditional areas or around older people, where even holding hands can feel forward. Follow your partner's lead — if they're reserved in public, match that.
