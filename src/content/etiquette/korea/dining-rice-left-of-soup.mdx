---
title: Place rice to the left of soup
country: korea
category: dining
severity: strict
summary: In Korean table settings, rice goes on your left and soup on your right, never switch them.
context: >
  Traditional Korean place settings follow a fixed layout (rice left, soup right,
  chopsticks and spoon on the right side of the setting). Swapping rice and soup
  is associated with ancestral memorial rites (jesa), where the arrangement is
  reversed, doing it at a regular meal reads as a bad omen to older generations.
source: Korean dining etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Set your bowl of rice on the left and your soup on the right. Spoon and chopsticks sit to the right of the setting, spoon on the outside.

This isn't just table dressing; the reversed layout (soup left, rice right) is specifically used at Korean memorial services for the deceased. Doing it at a normal meal can unsettle older relatives or hosts, even if younger Koreans today are more relaxed about it.
