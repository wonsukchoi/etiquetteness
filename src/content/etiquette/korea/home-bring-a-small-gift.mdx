Bring something modest: a box of fruit, bakery items, a small bouquet, or a nice tea or coffee set are all safe, common choices. If you know the household has kids, a small snack or toy for them is also well received.

Hand the gift over with both hands (or right hand supported at the wrist) once you're inside, not while still standing at the door. Don't expect the host to open it in front of you — it's normal for them to set it aside and open it later, in private.

- **Skip the usual taboo gifts** — sets of four, knives, clocks, white flowers, or shoes are all off the table here too.
- **Money is acceptable for some occasions** (housewarmings especially) but reads as too transactional for a casual dinner invite — save cash gifts for events where it's the norm, like weddings or moving in.
