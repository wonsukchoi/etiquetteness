Give and receive gifts with both hands (or right hand supported at the wrist by your left), same as with business cards or drinks. This part isn't optional even for a casual gift.

Once you've received it, say thank you, set it aside, and open it later once you're alone or the moment has passed, rather than unwrapping it on the spot. If the giver explicitly says "open it now," that overrides the default and it's fine to open it in front of them.

- **This is more flexible among close friends or younger Koreans** — many will open a gift immediately in casual settings without anyone thinking twice. It matters most with elders, first meetings, or formal occasions.
- **As the giver, don't push someone to open it in front of you** — let them decide, since pressing the point undercuts the reason for the norm in the first place.
