---
title: Bow and use two hands with seniors
country: korea
category: business
severity: strict
summary: Greet senior colleagues with a slight bow, and give or receive anything (cards, documents, drinks) with both hands, or your right hand supported at the wrist by your left.
context: >
  Korean workplace culture runs on a strict age/rank hierarchy (nunchi).
  A one-handed exchange with someone senior can read as careless or
  disrespectful even when it's unintentional, foreigners get some slack,
  but the gesture is noticed.
source: Korean business etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Bow slightly from the shoulders when greeting someone senior, depth of bow scales with the size of the age/rank gap. A handshake often follows in modern offices, but keep the bow too; don't swap one for the other.

Hand over business cards, documents, or a drink with both hands (or right hand with left hand lightly supporting your forearm). Receive the same way, and take a moment to look at whatever you're handed before setting it down, don't pocket it immediately.
