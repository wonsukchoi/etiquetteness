---
title: Turn away when drinking with elders
country: korea
category: social
severity: strict
summary: When drinking alcohol with someone older or more senior, angle your head and glass away from them as you drink, and pour for them with both hands before filling your own glass.
context: >
  Rooted in Confucian respect-for-elders norms that carried into modern
  Korean social and work drinking culture (hoesik). Drinking facing an
  elder directly, or pouring your own glass first, reads as disrespectful
  even if the elder doesn't say anything.
source: Korean social and drinking etiquette customs
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Never pour your own first drink at a table with elders, wait for someone to pour it for you, and pour theirs first when it's your turn, using both hands on the bottle (or one hand supported at the wrist).

When you actually drink, turn your head and glass slightly away from the senior person's line of sight rather than facing them directly. It's a small gesture, but it's the one people notice fastest when it's missing.
