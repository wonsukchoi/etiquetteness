Don't be surprised if an elevator panel skips straight from 3 to 5, or labels the fourth floor "F" — it's a deliberate substitution, not a building error.

When you have a free choice in how many of something to buy, arrange, or group (gift quantities, place settings, matching items), odd numbers like 3, 5, or especially 7 read as neutral-to-lucky, while 4 is worth avoiding if it costs nothing to do so.

- **This is a mild, background-level superstition, not an enforced rule** — nobody will call you out for it, and plenty of modern Koreans don't think about it day to day.
- **It compounds with the gift-giving taboo** — see the note on avoiding sets of four as gifts specifically, where the superstition carries more social weight than it does elsewhere.
