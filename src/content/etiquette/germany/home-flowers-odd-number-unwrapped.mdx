Pick a bouquet with an odd number of stems, three, five, seven, whatever fits the size you want, and take the paper wrapping off before you hand it to your host rather than presenting it still wrapped.

Steer clear of chrysanthemums, lilies, and carnations (funeral associations) and red roses (romantic, not host-gift territory) — tulips, gerberas, sunflowers, or light-colored roses are all safe defaults.

- **Hand the gift over right at arrival, not on your way out** — wine, quality chocolate, or flowers all work, and the timing matters as much as the item itself.
- **Don't wander past the room you're welcomed into** — guests are expected to stay where they're invited and not explore the rest of the home unprompted.
