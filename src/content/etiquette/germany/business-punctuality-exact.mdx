title: Arrive exactly on time, not early or late
country: germany

severity: strict
summary: Show up right at the scheduled time for a meeting — arriving more than a few minutes early can be as awkward as arriving late, since it can put your host on the spot before they're ready.
context: >
  Punctuality is treated as a sign of respect and reliability in German
  business culture. Being late without notice reads as genuinely
  unprofessional, more so than in many other cultures with a looser
  sense of "on time."

updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Aim to arrive within a couple of minutes of the scheduled time — not fifteen minutes early, and not late. If you're running behind by even a few minutes, call or message ahead rather than just showing up late unannounced.

This precision extends to meeting structure too: agendas are usually followed closely, and meetings tend to start and end on schedule.
