  German dating culture values directness and equality. People are
  known for being straightforward, even blunt, and value honesty and
  authenticity. Men and women are considered equal, either can ask the
  other out, and it's assumed you'll split the bill 50/50; when one
  person pays for the first date, the other usually pays for the second.
  Punctuality is a point of pride, and being late is a real turn-off.

Be clear about what you want. Germans appreciate honesty over hinting, so if you're interested, say so directly.

When the bill comes, expect to split it — it isn't a sign of disinterest. Arrive on time, or a few minutes early; keeping someone waiting is considered very disrespectful.
