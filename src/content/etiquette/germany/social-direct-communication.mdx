---
title: Don't mistake directness for rudeness
country: germany
category: social
severity: casual
summary: Blunt, direct feedback or opinions are normal in German conversation and aren't meant as a personal attack, the same directness in a country with more indirect norms might read as harsh.
context: >
  German communication culture generally values clarity and honesty over
  softened or indirect phrasing. Someone telling you plainly that an
  idea won't work, or that you made a mistake, is being efficient, not
  hostile.
source: "German Culture: Communication – Cultural Atlas"
sourceUrl: https://culturalatlas.sbs.com.au/german-culture/german-culture-communication
updatedAt: 2026-09-02
---

Expect direct "no"s, direct critique of ideas (not of you personally), and straightforward answers to direct questions, hedging or excessive small talk before getting to the point can read as evasive rather than polite.

If you're used to more indirect cultures, resist reading directness as anger or dislike; it's usually just how information gets exchanged efficiently.
